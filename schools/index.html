For teachers and staff

American Heart Association Heartsaver CPR AED and First Aid training for teachers, aides, coaches, administrators, and other school personnel. We can plan on-site sessions around professional development days or smaller staff groups.

  • Hands-on practice and skills evaluation
  • Course completion card for successful participants
  • Scheduling based on staff count and available space

For students

Hands-Only CPR and AED skills instruction with hands-on practice, planned by class, grade, or rotating stations. This is an instructional program; student certification cards are not included.

  • Age-appropriate practice with CPR manikins and AED trainers
  • Group size and timing tailored to the school
  • Designed to support Ohio high school CPR and AED instruction
