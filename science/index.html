What matters for training and readiness

Scientific statement

ACC / AHA · September 3, 2026

Cardiovascular readiness for tactical athletes

The AHA announcement covers a new joint statement addressing cardiovascular care for firefighters, EMS, law enforcement and other tactical professionals. It emphasizes emergency action plans and rapid AED access alongside individualized medical evaluation.

This is occupational cardiovascular guidance, not a replacement for CPR course materials or an individual fitness-for-duty determination. Summary based on the official announcement.

Evidence review · draft

ILCOR · Updated January 22, 2026

Adult cardiac-arrest vascular access

ILCOR revised its IV-versus-IO evidence review after an included trial was retracted. The document states that its overall conclusions did not change. The website labels this document as a draft; it is not a new standalone Valley Rescue Training protocol.

Read the ILCOR review →
Evidence review · draft

ILCOR · 2026 review

Temperature management after pediatric cardiac arrest

A draft systematic review evaluates temperature targets and treatment duration after pediatric cardiac arrest. It describes important uncertainty in the evidence. Follow applicable clinical protocols and medical direction; a draft review does not independently change PALS course requirements.

Review scope and limitations — September 17, 2026

This is a selected educational update, not an exhaustive literature review. The AHA CPR/ECC and first-aid guideline hubs, focused-update and highlights pages, ECC Digital Digest, AHA scientific-statements RSS feed, and ILCOR CoSTR listings were checked. Relevant documents summarized above were read. The AHA hubs continue to feature 2025 CPR/ECC guidance and 2024 first-aid guidance.

The ECC Digital Digest includes historical material; its older resources should not be confused with current course guidance. The AHA feed entries were screened for relevance, and the September 3 tactical-athlete announcement was reviewed.

Remaining limitation: Circulation’s latest-research listing could not be accessed, so a complete scan of its newest papers has not been verified. The tactical-athlete statement was verified through the official AHA announcement, not a full-text appraisal of the journal paper. No claim of a complete all-source review or instructor approval is made.

Updates are reviewed manually. New evidence does not automatically change clinical protocols or course requirements.

Educational information only: This page is not medical advice, a clinical protocol or a substitute for official guidelines, employer policies, medical direction or required course materials.
