How do I order equipment?

Use “Request this item” or “Get a quote” to email us with your quantity and pickup or shipping preference. We confirm availability, accessories and your final total, then provide payment instructions.

Pickup and shipping

Pickup or handoff at a scheduled class can be arranged at no shipping charge. Shipped orders are priced based on the items and destination, including cabinets and larger stations. We confirm shipping before you pay.

Stripe and payment options

We use Stripe for online payments. Eligible pay-over-time options may appear at checkout, subject to provider approval and terms. Available options vary by purchase and customer.

What is included with the FRx?

The listed AED includes a semi-rigid case, battery and one set of SMART Pads II. Ask about the infant/child key, spare pads, mounting and signage; we confirm your selected package before payment.

Does equipment include training?

Training is available separately. We can coordinate CPR/AED, first aid or bleeding-control training alongside your equipment order.
