How do I know which course my job or school requires?

Confirm the exact course and accepted delivery format with your employer or program before registering. If you share their requirements with us, we can help you identify the closest course option.

What is the difference between classroom and blended learning?

Classroom learning is completed with an instructor from start to finish. Blended learning combines an official online cognitive portion with a required hands-on skills session.
