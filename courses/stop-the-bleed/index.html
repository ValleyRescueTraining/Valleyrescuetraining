Stop the Bleed Training

What you will practice

Simple skills for critical moments

This hands-on course helps participants recognize serious bleeding and take immediate action using commonly available bleeding-control tools.

Recognize

Identify bleeding that may be life-threatening and understand when immediate action and emergency help are needed.

Apply pressure

Practice direct pressure and wound-packing techniques used to control severe bleeding.

Use a tourniquet

Learn when a tourniquet may be appropriate and practice correct application with training equipment.

Who should learn

Prepared people strengthen every organization

Workplaces and organizations

Useful for businesses, schools, churches, gyms, public agencies, safety teams and community organizations seeking practical emergency preparedness.

Responders and community members

Appropriate for anyone who wants the confidence to act during a bleeding emergency, with no clinical background required.
