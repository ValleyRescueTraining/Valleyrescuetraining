Choose your format

Classroom or blended learning

Both paths use American Heart Association course content and include hands-on skills. The right option depends on how you prefer to learn and schedule your training.

Fully in person

Classroom learning

An instructor leads the complete course in person, including discussion, demonstrations, practice and skills evaluation. This is best for learners who value immediate feedback throughout the class.

Online + hands-on

Blended learning

Complete the cognitive portion online at your own pace, then attend an in-person hands-on skills session. Bring your valid online completion certificate to the scheduled skills session.

Not sure which format your employer requires? Confirm their requirements before registering, or contact us and we will help you choose.

What to expect

Built for real-world readiness

Who it is for

  • Workplace and community responders
  • Teachers, coaches and childcare staff
  • Fitness, security and hospitality teams
  • Anyone who wants emergency-response skills

Core skills

  • Recognizing emergencies and getting help
  • CPR and safe AED use
  • First aid for common illnesses and injuries
  • Hands-on practice with instructor feedback

Course completion

Participants who successfully complete all required course components receive the applicable American Heart Association course completion card, valid for two years.
