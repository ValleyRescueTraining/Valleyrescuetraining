Pediatric emergency training

Pediatric Advanced Life Support

PALS training develops the assessment, recognition and team-response skills healthcare professionals use when caring for seriously ill or injured infants and children.

Course paths

Classroom and HeartCode options

Instructor-led

Classroom PALS

Complete the course through instructor-led learning, pediatric scenarios, hands-on skills and required evaluations. Initial and renewal options are coordinated according to learner needs.

Blended learning

HeartCode PALS

Complete the official adaptive online portion, followed by the required instructor-led hands-on skills session. A valid online completion certificate is required.

PALS is designed for healthcare professionals who respond to respiratory emergencies, shock and cardiopulmonary arrest in pediatric patients. Confirm employer requirements and required preparation before registering.

Course overview

Systematic pediatric assessment and response

Designed for

  • Physicians, nurses and advanced practice providers
  • Paramedics and other qualified professionals
  • Personnel involved in pediatric emergency care

Training focus

  • Systematic pediatric assessment
  • Respiratory emergencies and shock
  • High-quality resuscitation skills
  • Effective team dynamics and communication
