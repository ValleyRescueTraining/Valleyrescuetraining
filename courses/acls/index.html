HeartCode ACLS

Complete the official adaptive online portion, then complete the required hands-on skills session with an instructor. A valid online completion certificate is required.

ACLS is intended for healthcare professionals who direct or participate in the management of cardiopulmonary arrest and other cardiovascular emergencies. Required preparation and current BLS proficiency should be confirmed before class.

Course overview

Clinical decision-making and team response

Designed for

  • Physicians, nurses and advanced practice providers
  • Paramedics and other qualified professionals
  • Personnel involved in cardiovascular emergencies

Training focus

  • Systematic assessment and high-quality BLS
  • Cardiac arrest and peri-arrest management
  • Effective resuscitation team dynamics
  • Recognition and treatment of relevant rhythms
