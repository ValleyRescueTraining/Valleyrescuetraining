Course overview

Healthcare-focused resuscitation skills

Who it is for

  • Nurses, physicians and clinical staff
  • EMS and public-safety professionals
  • Healthcare and allied-health students
  • Other professionals whose role requires BLS

Training focus

  • High-quality CPR for adults, children and infants
  • AED use and effective ventilations
  • Recognition of life-threatening emergencies
  • Team dynamics and multi-rescuer response

Initial or renewal

Choose the option that matches your current credential and employer requirements. If you are unsure whether you qualify for renewal, contact us before registering.
