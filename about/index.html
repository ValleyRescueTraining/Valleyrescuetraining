PURPOSE BEHIND EVERY PRODUCT

We sell products we believe in.

Our work starts with training. The equipment we offer extends that same commitment to homes, workplaces and the people who serve our communities. We choose products with a clear role in emergency readiness and help customers understand what they’re buying.

Strategic partnerships. Shared purpose.

As a small business, we believe in working with other small businesses. We source from small-business suppliers and build strategic partnerships around products we believe in and a shared commitment to emergency readiness. Valley Rescue Training is an authorized retailer of LifeVac, offering Home, Travel and EMS kits alongside our emergency-readiness equipment.

LifeVac devices for the U.S. market are manufactured in the USA. We pair access to genuine products with clear kit information and links to the manufacturer’s current instructions.
