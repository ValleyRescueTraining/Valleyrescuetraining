Bleeding Control Knowledge Check

Review your understanding of severe bleeding and immediate-response priorities. Your answers are not saved or submitted.

1. Before helping someone with severe bleeding, what should you consider first?

Best answer: Protect yourself from hazards and activate emergency help while beginning appropriate bleeding control.

2. Which situation should make you concerned about life-threatening bleeding?

Best answer: Rapid, continuous or pooling blood can indicate a life-threatening emergency requiring immediate action.

3. A fundamental method for controlling external bleeding is:

Best answer: Firm direct pressure is one of the core bleeding-control actions taught by ACS Stop the Bleed.

4. Wound packing may be appropriate when:

Best answer: Wound packing is a hands-on technique for certain deep, severely bleeding wounds and should be learned through proper training.

5. A tourniquet is generally intended for:

Best answer: Tourniquets are used for life-threatening extremity bleeding when appropriate. Correct placement and tightening require hands-on practice.

6. After an effective tourniquet has been applied in an emergency, you should:

Best answer: Do not periodically loosen an emergency tourniquet. Make sure responding professionals know it was applied.

7. What are the three core bleeding-control actions emphasized by ACS Stop the Bleed?

Best answer: ACS Stop the Bleed teaches direct pressure, wound packing and tourniquet application through instruction and hands-on practice.

8. Why is hands-on bleeding-control training important?

Best answer: Hands-on practice lets you apply pressure, pack training wounds and use tourniquets with instructor feedback.
