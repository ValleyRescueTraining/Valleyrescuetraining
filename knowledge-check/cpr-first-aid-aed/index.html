CPR, First Aid & AED Knowledge Check

Choose the best answer for each situation. Your answers stay in this browser and are not saved or submitted.

1. An adult is unresponsive and is not breathing normally. What is the best response?

Best answer: Activate emergency response and begin CPR. Unresponsiveness with absent or abnormal breathing should be treated as cardiac arrest by a lay rescuer.

2. When should an AED be used for a person in suspected cardiac arrest?

Best answer: Use the AED as soon as it is available and follow its prompts while minimizing interruptions in CPR.

3. While an AED is analyzing the heart rhythm, rescuers should:

Best answer: Clear the person during rhythm analysis and any advised shock, then promptly follow the AED's next instruction.

4. What is most important when providing chest compressions?

Best answer: High-quality compressions and minimal interruptions are central to an effective response.

5. A person is having a seizure. What should you do?

Best answer: Protect the person from injury, do not restrain them and do not place anything in their mouth. Seek emergency help when appropriate.

6. What is an appropriate first step for a thermal burn after the danger is removed?

Best answer: Cool the burn appropriately; avoid direct ice and further tissue injury. The required care depends on severity, location and the person affected.

7. Someone has severe external bleeding. Your immediate priority is to:

Best answer: Severe bleeding requires immediate control and emergency activation. Use techniques and equipment within your training.

8. What does completing this online knowledge check demonstrate?

Best answer: A quiz can reinforce knowledge, but it cannot verify hands-on performance or provide course certification.
